WarehouseAI Tonight

From "I've got a groove" to "I've got a song sketch I can take with me."

WarehouseAI Tonight is a Windows desktop app for music producers. Drop song stems into its inbox before bed and it works through the queue overnight: it balances the stems, gently matches an optional reference track, finishes each song to a consistent listening level, and saves a WAV and an AAC copy.

If you connect Google Drive, Tonight uploads each night's AAC files to a dated folder in your own Drive so the demos are on your phone in the morning, ready for the car or earbuds.

Google Drive access

Tonight asks only for permission to create and manage the files it uploads itself (the drive.file scope). It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. You can disconnect it at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.
