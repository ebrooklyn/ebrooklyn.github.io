Privacy Policy

WarehouseAI Tonight · Last updated 30 October 2026

WarehouseAI Tonight ("Tonight") is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it does with your information.

What Tonight processes

Tonight processes the audio files you give it on your own computer. Your audio, mix settings and reports stay on your computer except for the files you choose to upload to your own Google Drive.

Google user data

  • Access requested: only the Google Drive drive.file scope, which lets Tonight create files and folders in your Drive and manage the files it created. It cannot read, list or change any other file in your Drive.
  • How it is used: solely to upload the demo files you queued, place them in a dated folder, and confirm each upload completed.
  • Storage: the sign-in token Google issues is stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows' per-user data protection. It is never sent to WarehouseAI or anyone else.
  • Sharing: Tonight does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Tonight's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No servers, no tracking

WarehouseAI runs no servers that receive your data. Tonight contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Removing access

You can revoke Tonight's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Uploaded files remain in your Drive until you delete them.
